## Candidate common business principles to harvest (proposal, not yet rule IDs)

1. **Evidence first; no invented attributes.** Title/description claims must be traceable to actual product-level or variant-level facts. Input title, legacy description, competitor copy, image OCR and SEO keywords are unverified context until substantiated. Do not invent size, color, material, weight, capacity, compatible models, safety, warranty, accessories, certifications, delivery promises, or performance.
2. **Identity before persuasion.** Start titles with the recognizable product identity/category and useful, verified distinguishing attributes. Avoid leading with promotional filler, operational identifiers, duplicated keywords, unsupported ranking/superlatives, or supplier metadata.
3. **Field-specific composition.** Title is a concise discovery/identification field; description is an organized decision-support narrative (overview, verified benefits/functions, specs, included components, applicable assembly/caution/delivery guidance). Operational warnings and assembly instruction should not leak into title.
4. **Completeness without fabrication.** Source-supported differentiators should not disappear between source pack, plan and final copy. Distinguish truly absent source data from extraction loss, planned exclusion, or generation loss. Missing critical facts should be flagged rather than padded with generic claims.
5. **Independent title / description lifecycle.** Generate, edit, accept, version and validate each field independently; changing one need not mutate the other. Both should share the same currently validated source snapshot, with change-aware stale detection where implemented.
6. **Platform-valid final text.** For Mercari Shops CREATE CSV, validate final `商品名` ≤130 characters and `商品説明` ≤3,000, **after** any prefix, boilerplate or export transformation. Do not silently truncate identity-critical information; preserve approved wording and reject when a safe reduction cannot be made.
7. **SEO evidence is not a product fact.** Competitor/category/search terms can guide term selection and order, but no competitor-specific attribute becomes a claim without product evidence. Avoid repetitive keyword stuffing; prefer natural Japanese.
8. **Deterministic quality gates before output.** Validate unsupported claims, title noise, duplicate/repeated blocks, character limits, source coverage and wrong-field leakage. An LLM score or human marketing preference cannot replace factual validation.
9. **Preserve operator edits and traceability.** User-approved content must not be silently overwritten by regeneration. Retain source version, generated version, prompt/model when relevant, accepted version, override and reason.
10. **Do not conflate design with deployed policy.** A POC or planned model may supply a method worth harvesting, but platform facts, business decisions, deployed behavior and unverified hypotheses must be distinctly labeled.

## Known conflicts / unresolved decisions

- **One-step generation vs typed Copy Plan:** V4's narrow component realization is an experimentally informed architecture; independent Giga CSV app has distinct product boundaries. Business policy should require evidence/quality outcomes, not mandate the same implementation or LLM.
- **Preserve-first vs rewrite:** Preserve validated facts and operator-approved edits. The exact amount of rewriting and the target title structure remain a copy strategy decision, not a universal fixed template.
- **SEO strength:** No evidence in this review establishes a measured conversion uplift or company-wide keyword-density rule. Neither a minimum title length nor a mandatory set of SEO keywords is ready for canonical business rule status.
- **Claim-strengthening / marketing language:** Deferred in commerce-lab #111. Do not imply an LLM judge is a required production gate.
- **Source truth:** Supplier name/title, image-derived inference, descriptions, official product specification and operator verification can conflict. Define source-level provenance and conflict escalation before declaring a claim verified.
- **Runtime ownership:** Multiple generators exist across repos. This research does not authorize a new cross-repo copy service, Canonical migration or changes to product scope.

## Recommended harvest disposition

- **High confidence, suitable for sponsor approval:** evidence-grounded claims; final field limits; no SKU-code or source-plan leakage; independent title/description edit states; no silent override of approved edits; accurate product identity.
- **Candidate for further controlled testing:** structured description sections, keyword selection/ranking, title opening order, optional source-ID coverage checks and various prompting styles.
- **Do not canonicalize yet:** model/provider choice, strict title length target below 130, keyword quotas, universal marketing-language scoring, fixed description template, automatic competitor imitation, historical Shop4-specific audit thresholds.
