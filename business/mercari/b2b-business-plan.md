## 1. Executive strategy and business objective

**Decision / positioning:** Shop4 is the **primary consumer-facing storefront and lead-entry point** for a lightweight, separately measured **B2B purchase-support service**, *not* a new legal entity, a standalone B2B shop or a new app at launch.

**Value proposition:** Make it easier for Japanese businesses and facilities to purchase multiple units of furniture, storage, office/venue fixtures and selected care-related goods by providing **quantity quotations, consolidated truck-shipping assessment, practical delivery coordination and willingness to discuss special ordinary commercial documentation**.

**Customer promise:** *“法人・事業者様のまとめ買い、トラック便の一括配送、特別な購入書類のご相談を承ります。条件に応じて個別に対応可否・費用・納期をご案内します。”* No guaranteed discount, dispatch date or document issuance.

**Registration fact (management-provided, as of 2026-11-14):** リテルパルス合同会社 **is not registered as an 適格請求書発行事業者**. Thus Shop4 **cannot issue an 適格請求書 (qualified tax invoice)** while unregistered. Ordinary **non-qualified** commercial receipts/invoices may be evaluated as special service upon Backoffice approval, but are not automatically provided. Standard orders use Mercari confirmation/order-detail/payment statements. Separate routine and exceptional cases per [SOP](buyer-receipt-invoice-sop.md).

### Why test this business?

The 2026-11-14 operational study observed **6,052** order lines, **141** weak *OR*-screened business-address/name candidates (including **2 own-company shipments** that must be excluded), and **206** bulk-tagged inquiries. These are **not** validated unique business buyers or a measured B2B market size. Among candidate orders furniture (58) and storage (40) dominate the crude product-title classification. Examples of commercial **delivery locations** include nursing/care, child-development and hospital facilities; receipt at a facility does **not** establish that the institution paid.

The Shop4 ten-set dining-furniture truck example quoted supplier parcel freight **¥18,920** vs truck plus loading **¥6,300**, a **¥12,620 / 66.7% *quoted*** difference on an excluding-packaging basis; actual logistics savings, fulfillment time and paid conversion were not verified. Use it to demonstrate **potential** freight value, not an advertised 66.7% guarantee.

## 2. Target segments, jobs-to-be-done, and eligibility

| Segment hypothesis | Likely customer job | Candidate SKUs | Strength of current evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurants, cafes, small hospitality operators | Multiple matching tables/chairs, freight estimate, receive together | Dining sets/chairs, storage, freezers | Shop4 10-set truck inquiry; industry identity **not** proven |
| Nursing, day-care, child-development and medical facilities | Fit-out furniture, storage, mobility/activities supplies, receiving coordination | Chairs, lockers, walkers, wheelchairs, indoor play/therapy fixtures | Actual **facility-address deliveries** observed; paying entity often unknown |
| Offices, retailers and other small businesses | Simple consolidated procurement for offices/retail premises | Desks, filing cabinets, shelves, lockers | Name/address signals and merchandise orders; exact buyer still unverified |
| Industrial, logistics, auto and service sites | Larger fixtures and storage, coordinated delivery | Storage sheds, cabinets, site furniture | Several organization/location cross-checks; actual buyer role varies |

**Eligibility at quote time:** Product legally sellable and appropriately described; stock and sourcing confirmed; specific quantity, destination prefecture/city, delivery constraints and required receiving date supplied; price can support an approved contribution margin. Include business-paperwork needs early so a buyer is **not surprised after payment**. Do not infer medical-purpose suitability/certification from a destination hospital.

## 3. Offer design and operating boundaries

| Offer | Standard Shop4 experience | Enhanced B2B request (subject to approval) |
|---|---|---|
| Quantity | Listed unit or prepackaged set | Review larger order size and optional dedicated approved listing/quote |
| Freight | Existing listing delivery rules | Compare standard parcel and truck; separately include packing, palletization, loading and doorstep/inside-delivery limitations |
| Lead time | Actual listing dispatch window | Reconfirm warehouse prep, truck booking and transit separately; no fixed arrival promise without supplier evidence |
| Documentation | Purchase-confirmation email, Mercari order details, payment-provider statements | Assess buyer-requested **ordinary, non-qualified** receipt, company addressee or purchase statement case by case; Backoffice approval before promising |
| Qualified tax invoice | Not issued | **Not available while not registered**; no T registration number or qualified invoice claims |
| Follow-up | Respond to buyer's Inquiry and order-related communications | Future, *permissioned* business-lead management only; no unsolicited platform messaging or data export |
| Catalog | Existing public listings | Future request-driven curated product selection / quotation; not a universal public supplier catalog |

**No platform bypass:** Keep customer purchase and payment on approved Mercari flows. Do not solicit bank transfer or off-platform contacts through Mercari messages. Shop4 remains the merchant-of-record for offers within the company's scope.

## 4. Go-to-market — Shop4 main storefront

### 4.1 Shop Profile: primary B2B discovery message — Phase 1

**Placement:** Shop4's current \`ショップ説明\` alongside its existing policies, not replacing delivery/returns details. Review the entire live profile before publishing; reconcile delivery timing with actual listing promises. Owner/admin can edit from **Settings → ショップ情報の編集** per Mercari official guide.

**Proposed Japanese text (short):**

> 【法人・事業者様のまとめ買い歓迎】
>
> 飲食店・オフィス・介護施設・各種事業所など、複数点のご購入もお気軽にご相談ください。
> 商品・数量・配送先に応じて、トラック便による一括配送や配送料のお見積もり、納品条件、特別な購入書類のご要望について個別に確認いたします。
>
> ※対応可否・配送料・納期は商品や配送地域により異なります。適格請求書（インボイス）の発行には対応しておりません。

**Operational action:** Verify current profile, draft the insertion, owner reviews and publishes using approved seller session. Not executed by this document.

**Acceptance:** Live profile shows B2B CTA while retaining existing returns/dispatch obligations; no assertion that qualified invoices can be issued.

### 4.2 Selected listing description boilerplate — Phase 1

**Selection principles:** Start with **Shop4**, prioritizing live stocked merchandise with natural multi-unit or facility use:
- matching dining furniture, multi-chair sets, tables;
- office desks, lockers, cabinets and storage racks;
- outdoor/storage/shelter and larger commercial fixtures where truck freight may improve unit economics;
- appropriate care/day-service goods, **without unsupported medical or safety certification claims**.

Do **not** paste into every listing, automatically change titles or promise truck freight for single lightweight items. Keep original product specifications and safety/dispatch instructions intact. Append near the end of selected \`商品説明\`, respecting Mercari's description-length limit and approved listing CSV guideline.

**Japanese boilerplate (standard):**

> 【法人・事業者様／まとめ買いのご相談】
> 店舗・オフィス・介護施設・各種事業所などでの複数点購入をご検討の場合は、購入前にお問い合わせください。
>
> ご希望の商品・数量・お届け先の都道府県／市区町村をお知らせいただければ、お見積もりやトラック便などによる一括配送の可否・配送料・納期をご確認いたします。
> 領収書など特別な購入書類に関するご希望も、内容に応じて個別に対応可否をご案内します。
>
> ※在庫・配送地域・配送条件によりご希望に添えない場合があります。当ショップでは適格請求書（インボイス）を発行できません。

**Targeted variant for nursing / care listings** (do not imply medical-device compliance):

> 【施設・事業所様のまとめ買い相談】
> 介護施設・デイサービス・療育施設などでのご利用や複数点のご購入について、必要数量・納品先地域・納品時期を添えて購入前にお問い合わせください。お見積もり・配送方法・特別な購入書類のご希望を個別に確認いたします。
> ※商品の仕様・用途は商品説明をご確認ください。納期・書類対応は確約できません。適格請求書（インボイス）は発行できません。

**Rollout:** Review a manually selected small set first (proposed first batch **10–20 Shop4 listings**, not a mandate or existing confirmed inventory), verify item applicability and product-description length, then publish through existing authorized update process. Track selected product IDs and dates in operating SSOT, not this plan.

**Acceptance:** On selected listings, a buyer can recognize the business option and knows what to provide for a quote; no false guarantee of lower freight, bulk discount or document issuance.

### 4.3 Optional Shop4 Newsletter — Phase 1B (conditional)

**Use case:** One test issue featuring a *small coherent selection* of business-friendly products (“店舗・施設向けの家具・収納特集”), with the primary CTA inviting pre-purchase quantity/delivery inquiries.

**Official delivery constraints (verified 2026-11-14):**
- Recipient audience is **customers subscribed to this Shop's newsletter**, **not** the inferred B2B candidate list; **do not assume business-only targeting/segmentation is supported**.
- Max **4 newsletters per month**, scheduled at **08:00–22:00** in **30-minute increments**, reservation up to **two weeks ahead**; no immediate send.
- Up to **10 listed products** per issue; draft items cannot be selected. Seller guide supports up to **2,000** plain-text body characters (no HTML/Markdown), **60** in subject and **40** in heading.
- Promotional content must comply with platform rules. Legal merchant details are disclosed in sent emails under applicable Japanese rules.

**Proposed test content:** 5–10 currently stocked Shop4 furniture/storage/office/care-use products plus short business-quotation CTA, no independent invoice guarantee. **Optional**: do not consume scarce newsletter slots without credible inventory, product selection, delivery economics and owner approval.

**Draft Japanese subject:** \`【店舗・施設向け】まとめ買い・一括配送のご相談\`

**Draft Japanese body:**

> 店舗・オフィス・介護施設などでご利用いただける家具・収納商品をご紹介します。
> 複数点の購入や同一住所への一括配送をご希望の場合は、ご希望の商品・数量・お届け先地域を添えて購入前にご相談ください。商品・地域によってはトラック便もお見積もりいたします。
> ※送料・納期・書類対応は個別確認となり、適格請求書は発行できません。

**Measurement:** Gross inquiries referring to newsletter, qualified B2B leads, quotes, eventual paid orders, incremental contribution; do not assume Mercari provides lead-level attribution. Use buyer self-identification and timestamp context where permitted.

## 5. Commercial funnel and owner workflow

| Stage | Owner | Event / input | Output / acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| DISCOVERED | Shop4 Ops | Profile CTA, selected listing, optional newsletter | Customer chooses existing Inquiry |
| QUALIFYING | Shop4 Support | Item/SKU, number of *sets* vs included units, destination prefecture/city, intended date, unloading constraints, paperwork needs | Customer's real stated requirements; no speculative industry assumption |
| SUPPLIER_QUOTE | Procurement/Giga | Stock, standard delivery, truck feasibility, pallet/packing/handling and seller arrangements | Date-bound supplier quote and lead-time *components* |
| COMMERCIAL_REVIEW | Shop4 Ops | Product cost, marketplace commission, shipping/handling and contribution | Reviewed buyer offer; record whether shipping is charged separately |
| OFFERED | Shop4 Support | Quote validity, actual delivery scope, special documentation result | Buyer-facing confirmed terms / optional approved dedicated Mercari listing |
| PAID | Shop4 Ops | Verified buyer payment/order ID | Purchase released; no supplier purchase solely on unaccepted inquiry |
| FULFILLING | Procurement/Ops | Order, warehouse prep, truck booking, dispatch | Delivery and exceptions tracked |
| CLOSED | Ops + Backoffice | Actual freight settlement, final buyer charge, receipt request resolution | Contribution measured; no unverified margin/savings claims |

**Example operating principle:** a 10-set dining order may contain 10 retail packages or fewer packaged sets. Distinguish \`order.quantity\`, the product's own set size and number of physical boxes. Confirm customer expectations before quote.

**Supplier lead-time warning:** Giga's example 1–3 *working days* is warehouse preparation only, not final delivery; truck booking and transit may be unknown. Do not propagate historical quote validity or guaranteed five-day delivery.

## 6. Phase 2 — Buyer identification and proactive business follow-up (TO-BE)

**Objective:** identify genuinely *business-operated* demand from existing buyer interactions and orders, then support repeat commercial procurement where platform functionality, contact permissions and data-protection requirements allow it.

**Status:** **To-be concept, not enabled.** Current evidence does **not** prove that Mercari permits initiating unsolicited promotional messages to all historical buyers. Official messaging guide is principally inquiry replies and order-related contact; order notices are **one-way** and not a substitute for marketing. Newsletter only reaches subscribers.

**Future minimum workflow:** 
1. Build *internal* candidate segments **without outreach**: self-declared business buyer/explicit bulk inquiry; matched public business **location** (not verified payer); buyer name/address heuristic; potential repeated business. Preserve evidence levels.
2. Cross-check against order status, refunds, business ownership, Shop3 boundaries, and buyer's actual intent. **Do not auto-merge distinct customers by name/address**.
3. Identify permitted channel, proof of consent / buyer-initiated engagement and purpose limitation. Obtain legal/privacy and Mercari policy verification **before proactive promotional contact**.
4. Where authorized, use a minimal human-reviewed *reorder or business-support follow-up*, centered on the buyer's expressed need (quote expiry, requested restock, agreed repeat purchase) rather than mass unsolicited messages.
5. Log consent/channel/evidence/owner/result in authorized operations system; provide opt-out where relevant.

**Release gate:** verified buyer identity and permissions, documented permitted channel, approved message template and data retention, test cohort and audit. **If outbound marketing is not allowed, retain inbound enquiry + newsletter subscribers only.**

**KPIs (future):** verified buyer entities, qualified demand, approved outreach rate, response/quote/paid conversion, repeat contribution. Do not represent 141 heuristic order lines as contactable corporate accounts.

## 7. Phase 3 — On-demand Product Catalog (TO-BE)

**Objective:** support business buyers who ask for related furniture, multiple items, size/color combinations or goods not currently listed on Shop4, by providing a *request-driven curated selection* and consolidated quote.

**Not yet a public catalog product, full Giga dump, or customer-facing app.** A future catalog should be **derived from existing canonical product/image/listing and supplier evidence**, while Shop4 remains the Mercari checkout channel. Do not create parallel catalog SSOT or promise that unlisted products are in stock.

**Manual discovery MVP first:** Buyer submits need (category, purpose, specs, quantities, target budget if offered, delivery region/date). Operator researches approved supplier SKUs, confirms availability, commercial applicability, images/copy rights and truck constraints, and returns a narrow shortlist with an approved quote or Mercari listing. Do not share supplier confidential costs or off-platform payment links.

**Future minimal data model (conceptual, not approved migration):**
- \`b2b_request\` (request id, shop4 identity, buyer-stated specs, quantities, destination region, due date, stage)
- \`b2b_request_item\` (candidate canonical product/variant/item code, qty, fit note, availability check, seller offer)
- \`b2b_quote\` (item lines, pricing, stock check timestamp, truck/parcel quote validity, lead-time assumptions, approved margin)
- \`b2b_offer\` (approved Shop4 listing reference; paid order link and actual fulfillment reconciliation)
- \`b2b_contact_permission\` (channel permission and scope, only if legally/operationally justified)

**Systems policy:** reuse existing Commerce Catalog & Listing and operational SSOT; create only necessary additive state after product-design review. **No canonical product, image or listing migration** is part of this business plan. No new portal or UI is authorized here.

**Release gate:** repeated documented unmet requests, achievable sourcing/quoting economics, stock/offer ownership and versioned quotes, customer-safe delivery process and channel rules verified.

## 8. Commercial KPIs, economics and stage gates

**Measure a separate Shop4 B2B *operating segment* (not new accounting legal entity):**
- **Demand:** business-intent inquiries / month, quote rate, accepted quote, paid order rate, verified business buyer identity (buyer self-declared vs verified).
- **Value:** gross order basket, product quantities *within a set*, ordered sets, recurring buyer purchase, average verified B2B basket vs ordinary Shop4 retail.
- **Unit economics:** Shop4 sale amount; supplier item cost and shipping/loading/pallet fees; Mercari marketplace fee; incremental handling cost; *contribution* (not gross sales alone).
- **Shipping:** quoted standard vs truck freight and actual settlement; never conflate supplier shipping cost and customer charged freight.
- **Service:** enquiry-to-quote, quote-to-payment, warehouse prep, booking, dispatch-to-delivery; delivery exceptions and special document approval outcome.
- **Marketing:** number of selected B2B listings, attributed inquiry counts (where actually traceable), newsletter subscribers/orders without inventing segmentation, consented follow-up performance (Phase 2).

**Decision gates:**
- **P0 — Design approved**: Shop4 positioning, wording, eligible-product rules, support owners and tax/documentation checks accepted; no live changes until approved.
- **P1 — Launch storefront copy only**: shop profile and selected listing description boilerplate published and verified; existing fulfillment, Inquiry and payment process unchanged.
- **P1B — Optional newsletter**: run at most one small test after eligible inventory, subscriber audience, calendar slot and expected benefit checked.
- **P2 — Buyer-aware follow-up**: separate platform policy/legal approval + evidence-based buyer eligibility + controlled consent/contact tracking.
- **P3 — On-demand catalog**: documented unmet commercial demand, supplier selection evidence, quote and listing linkage; separate technical design approved.

**Do not invent revenue targets or expected B2B conversion** until a verified paid cohort, valid true-basket calculations and actual shipping settlements exist.

## 9. Governance, risks and decision backlog

| Risk / blocker | Handling | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer is person at business address, not business purchaser | Keep location and payer identities separate; do not infer company turnover from deliveries | Business Development / Ops |
| Invoice / documentation expectation | Platform records first; special ordinary documents reviewed; **no 適格請求書 while not registered** | Backoffice |
| Freight discount / delivery promise not real | Giga quote per order, document loading/packaging, use actual settlement and final delivery timestamps | Procurement |
| Listing copy differs from standard shipping terms | Check each selected product's shipping and legal claims before publish | Shop4 Ops |
| Marketing contact permissions / Mercari limits | Phase 2 blocked pending official rule review and consent pathway | Ops / Compliance |
| Newsletter reach not business-only | Opt-in Shop4 audience only; no assumed segmentation | Shop4 Ops |
| Shop3 merchandise income treated as our turnover | Separate legal ownership and demand-only analysis | Finance |
| Product catalog scope creep | On-demand shortlist first; reuse canonical product data, no parallel data SSOT | Product Owner |

**Business decisions recorded in this draft:** Shop4 primary acquisition/fulfillment channel; platform-first documents with potential exception service; newsletter optional; proactive buyer follow-up and on-demand catalog explicitly future/to-be. **Approval still required** for production listing/profile edits, outbound campaigns, any document issuance, customer outreach and any software/database build.
